Designers now acknowledge that partitions will inevitably occur, even if rarely. The emphasis is on building systems that can detect partitions and react accordingly, therefore, strategies exist to handle partitions, such as entering a "partition mode" that limits operations and prioritizes recovering from inconsistencies. Realizations of this functionality exist in the form of intentional delays of backend operations to increase the perception of availability to the front-end user, supported with CRDT (commutative replicated data types) that allow for facile merging of operations from partitions that existed at different states prior to recovery. The goal of these implementations is to maximize a combination of consistency and availability that works for the application.
For instance, a social media feed might prioritize availability over absolute consistency during a partition, allowing users to post updates even if they might not be immediately visible to everyone.
