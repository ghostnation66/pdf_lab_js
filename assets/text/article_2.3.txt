Multi-Version Concurrency Control (MVCC): Spanner utilizes MVCC, a technique that maintains historical versions of data. This allows reads to access a consistent view of the data even if updates are ongoing, further bolstering consistency. MVCC effectively allows Spanner to maintain snapshots of state versions (similar to the index/commit feature of Git). The concept of monotonically increasing versions for each consecutive snapshot enables enables Spanner to attain a sustainable form of eventual consistency that is conducive to improved concurrency (allows multiple transactions to read and write data concurrently, improving overall system performance and scalability), reduced deadlocks (Since MVCC doesn't rely on exclusive locking, it significantly reduces the risk of deadlocks, which are situations where two or more transactions are waiting for each other to release locks), and non-blocking reads (read operations don't need to wait for write operations to complete, further improving performance)
