Google's vast private network minimizes partition occurrences. Google operates an internally managed private global network which hosts the Spanner service. Each data center typically has at least three independent fibers connecting it to the private global network, thus ensuring path diversity for every pair of data centers; redundancy of equipment also exists as a contingency against catastrophic events, such as cut lines, outages, and partition failures. Additionally, the services is made more robust against updates that may potentially break multiple paths simultaneously, and deleterious effects are mitigated so that some paths remain active despite a bad update or reconfiguration.
